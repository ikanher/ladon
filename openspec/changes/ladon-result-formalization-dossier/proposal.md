## Why

The fixed-epoch exposition now resolves all 25 supplied Lean targets, but resolution alone does not explain the nine conventional-only components, the two unchecked implication adapters, or the scope of stored candidate checking. A reader needs those distinctions next to the exact claim and formal statement.

## What Changes

- Add offline `ladon result inspect` over an explicit result manifest and selected canonical evidence, with claim/target selection, bounded cards and revision-bound continuation.
- Add a separately versioned, revision-bound component-assessment companion. Keep the strict manifest v1 contract unchanged and preserve supplier attribution and historical assessments.
- Reuse the canonical resolution, stored receipt, theorem dossier and lineage owners; show separate assumption origins, coverage, correspondence and checking dimensions.
- Exercise installed inspection on the completed real exposition capture, preserving its original evidence and correspondence limitations. Detailed acceptance is in `specs/ladon-result-formalization-dossier/spec.md`.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `ladon-result-formalization-dossier`: bounded offline inspection of exact result claims, component coverage and existing formal evidence, implementing the owning umbrella's capability.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Existing ProofIR identity, attachment and observation authority contracts remain authoritative.

## Impact

Additive Python result CLI and projection modules, a packaged companion schema, tests and documentation. No runtime dependencies, new ProofIR artifact families, implicit Lean execution, index refresh or network access.
