# `ladon-project-ownership-and-generated-calibration`

Owns target-authored, target-configured-generated, Lean/compiler-generated, and
imported-dependency populations plus inspectable generated-family provenance
and family-collapsed review views.

- Existing owners reused: text declaration authority, elaborated declaration
  rows, generated attribution, raw population labels, and policy discovery.
- Enables: owner-relevant finding ranking and audit-command resolution.
- Excludes: target-specific `Data`/`Row` hard-coding, source rewriting,
  generator correctness claims, or deletion of raw full-projection evidence.
- Exit: positive/negative fixtures keep authored declarations visible, suppress
  compiler artifacts from authored family promotion, preserve compact imported
  identities for edges, and expose every generated classification reason.
