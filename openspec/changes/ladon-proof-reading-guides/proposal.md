## Why

The qualified dossier exposes exact formal evidence but does not explain how to read a proof or why a supporting lemma matters. The next umbrella child lets readers follow supplied explanations and citations while keeping authorship, review currency and formal evidence separate.

## What Changes

- Add a separately versioned reading-guide companion with ordered, revision-bound steps, exact claim/target/source references and attributed citation assertions. Manifest v1 remains unchanged.
- Add offline `ladon result guide` with exact selectors and bounded continuation; reuse existing dossier, lineage and derivation observations for supporting navigation.
- Preserve independent explanation, correspondence and attribution reviews, including disputes and historical approvals after edits.
- Exercise the installed command on the immutable fixed-epoch exposition, beginning with its condition, counterexample and release scopes. Detailed acceptance is in `specs/ladon-proof-reading-guides/spec.md`.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `ladon-proof-reading-guides`: authored reading paths, attributable literature relationships and exact lemma-reuse navigation, implementing the existing umbrella capability.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Existing canonical identity, dossier and structural evidence owners retain their authority contracts.

## Impact

Additive result companion schema, Python validation/projection modules, ordinary CLI integration, tests and documentation. No runtime dependencies, implicit model or Lean execution, downloads, index refresh or new ProofIR families. The full exit requires compatible dossier and upstream receipts. Portable bundles and later evaluation remain separate children.
