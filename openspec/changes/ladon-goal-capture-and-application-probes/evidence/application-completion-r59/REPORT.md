# Captured-original-goal completion (r59)

Tasks 1.3 and 2.3 are qualified against candidate `720d28439c4a3a1178163af3b116129cc7ed743e`. The ordinary `proof-search goal complete` CLI consumes a complete successful source-capture envelope and a whole proposed term. It re-observes the original goal and ordered context, checks the term there, and requires independent temporary Lean compilation plus complete transitive axiom evidence before reporting `completed`. Earlier exploration acceptance, scratch receipts and handwritten goals cannot supply completion evidence.

The new helper preserves actual local definition values rather than generalizing them into hypotheses. Internal/self dependencies through local types or values are rejected. Accepted closed expressions have no expression/level metavariables or free variables and must roundtrip in an empty local context. The independent compiler collects dependencies of the generated declaration. Policy `lean-standard-no-placeholders-v1` permits `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` and `propext`; `sorryAx` and other axioms prevent completion. Compiler exit zero alone is insufficient. This closes the selected goal under the observed context, not the enclosing unfinished production declaration.

The initial profile remains ordinary non-modular source with default frontend setup. Source-local constants unavailable in compiled imports can prevent replay. Contexts containing unresolved expression/level metavariables are unavailable. Known local definitions remain part of the closed context, so their dependencies can conservatively affect trust even when the proposed term does not mention them. Failure or unavailability does not establish that a mathematical claim is false.
