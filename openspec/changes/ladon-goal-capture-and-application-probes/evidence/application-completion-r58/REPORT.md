# Captured-goal completion feasibility (r58)

A bounded scratch prototype supports one imported-context route: reprocess the source to recover a selected post-constructor goal, elaborate `h` in its actual context, close the ordered noninternal locals, roundtrip the closed target and term with empty locals, namespace and open declarations, then replay those exact strings in a separate ordinary Lean compiler process. The fixture contains dependent locals, a local instance, an actual dependent `let`, a nondependent `have` and two selectable goals. A type-correct application of the internal `_example` local is explicitly rejected rather than becoming a proof premise. Independent transitive axiom collection returned `[]`.

Root verified the final source/helper/replay hashes and full successful receipts, including the rejected self-dependency control. The last helper used 2.785 seconds and 3,731,468,288 bytes peak process-tree RSS; independent replay used 0.467 seconds and 1,582,845,952 bytes. Continuation processes have 30-second, 32 GiB, 8 MiB limits. Recorded cumulative supervised Lean time is approximately 34.259 seconds of the 120-second feasibility budget, including failures and root's corrected-position attempt; this is an estimate, not a complete reconstructed telemetry population.

Earlier negative results included a wrong source position and scratch instrumentation errors. The wrong-position conclusion was corrected, not treated as proof that the API route fails. Two original short failure receipts were overwritten by the worker; preserved bounded reproductions are labelled as reproductions and do not reconstruct those original runs. The initial worker used 8 GiB caps; continuation uses the authorized 32 GiB cap. Board evidence records these limitations.

The nondependent `have z` was generalized into a parameter of the closed replay, and the positive term was `h`, not `z`. This does not demonstrate faithful replay of a term that depends on that local definition's actual value. The required admitted-dependency control must account for used local values and their dependencies; checking the closed wrapper alone could miss a placeholder hidden behind such a parameter. This is a source-based limitation of the positive fixture, not a reported failing run.

This is feasibility evidence only. It does not complete tasks 1.3 or 2.3, qualify a production CLI, prove all source-local constants can replay, or establish the trust policy's admitted/allowed-axiom/missing-coverage outcomes. Next: freeze that completion contract and its negative controls before integrating production code.
