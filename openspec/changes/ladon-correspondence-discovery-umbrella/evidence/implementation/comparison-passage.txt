10.4. Growth, lifespan, and viscosity. The force extension and energy estimate are now
established for the localized fields (u, p) (Lemma 10.3, (10.13)). In this subsection we use the
inner growth asymptotic (3.6) from Theorem 3.1 to identify the maximal classical lifespan
and, with the comparison lemma (Lemma 10.5), exclude a global smooth solution of bounded
energy. A spatial rescaling then gives the result for every fixed positive viscosity without
changing the singular time.
