# Work-package briefs

These briefs describe the umbrella's four packages. They are not separate implementation changes and have no independent completion counters; [tasks.md](tasks.md) owns progress. Runtime children are conditional and must link back to the relevant [requirements](specs/ladon-correspondence-discovery/spec.md).

## A. Source coverage and triage

**Consumer:** an auditor deciding what to inspect next and a reader deciding what the audit actually covered.

**Inputs:** frozen original exposition, blind and post-reveal findings, exact target revision. Use the surrounding Lemma 8.6 and Lemma 10.5 passages as known development ranges, plus faithful examples. Diagnosis is disclosed for these runs.

**Deliver:** a readable inventory of assertions and reasoning uses; selected and unexamined spans; extraction uncertainties; candidate formal counterparts; a queue ranked by mathematical consequence and missing evidence. Distinguish explicit numbered claims from model-identified implicit steps. A second inventory pass checks omissions independently of successful formal lookup.

**Exit evidence:** the inventory includes the two estimates and their consumers, accounts for every selected paragraph by review status, and still shows unmapped or unexamined content when all chosen formal targets resolve. PDF/TeX notation ambiguity is resolved from the source or kept open.

**Potential child admission:** existing views cannot display the source population and its omissions in the consuming audit. A proposed inventory helper must show the failing example and retain authored inventory authority; it cannot promise automatic completeness.

## B. Quantitative contract comparison

**Consumer:** an auditor checking whether an existing formal estimate supports the exact claim the exposition needs.

**Inputs:** A's assertions, exact declaration types and definitions, the derivative-loss and pressure-flux cases, faithful/equivalent controls.

**Deliver:** side-by-side contracts following design decision 3, the proposed implication direction and a statement of missing premises or strengthening. Keep statement and method findings separate and attribute every interpretation. Explain the mathematical significance of each difference rather than merely highlighting changed tokens.

**Exit evidence:** reproduce both discrepancies at the cited revision, reject invalid implication directions, and avoid false alarms for equivalent notation, valid normalization and a genuinely stronger supporting theorem. A changed method alone is not a false-proof finding.

**Potential child admission:** manually inspected exact types are unavailable or incorrectly represented by a current reader, or repeated comparison requires a specific supported normalization. A child must preserve original expressions, identity and unsupported cases; a general semantic diff engine is not presumed.

## C. Consumer-directed local checking

**Consumer:** a mathematician deciding whether to accept, revise or leave open one step of the original argument.

**Inputs:** B's original local obligations and existing source-goal/candidate checking facilities; actual proof bodies and premise-producing constructions.

**Deliver:** small checked adapters or precise unresolved obligations, original and revised goal records, proof-method comparisons, and an exercised reader-facing recipe. Use existing results; do not duplicate the full formalization. Include controls where the final theorem checks but the purported intermediate statement does not, and where a premise-bearing structure has a valid concrete producer.

**Exit evidence:** a successful endpoint cannot discharge an unexamined intermediate claim; a changed goal is visible; a failed Lean attempt is not classified as a refutation; a concrete counterexample has its own checked statement or conventional proof. Readable findings remain usable without manufacturing canonical evidence.

**Potential child admission:** a specific local obligation cannot retain its original context, revision or comparison evidence through an existing operation. The child owns its compatibility design, tests, docs and acceptance output. Link its result here without copying its task list; no child is admitted solely to increase command usage.

## D. Withheld discovery evaluation

**Consumer:** Ladon maintainers deciding which workflow or implementation improvements have demonstrated value.

**Inputs:** frozen A–C workflow, a separate curator's 12-excerpt pilot and two longer selections, isolated evaluation inputs and a prespecified scoring rubric. The curator's reference answers are unavailable to the auditing contexts.

**Deliver:** separate ordinary-tool and Ladon-assisted results, contamination checks, case-level localization and classification, false alarms, missed claims, unresolved work, measured costs and a supported benefit decision. Keep naturally occurring and authored cases separate.

**Exit evidence:** every planned run is accounted for; hidden labels stayed inaccessible or the run is explicitly disqualified; all failures and interventions remain visible. The known Navier–Stokes cases contribute no withheld score. A negative or inconclusive pilot is a complete evaluation result and does not authorize an automatic retest.

**Potential child admission:** only a concrete evaluation operation missing from existing supervision/recording justifies a helper. Do not build a benchmark platform or new model-service dependency. If isolated evaluation cannot be run with available tooling, retain the precise limitation and leave the detection-benefit claim unmet.
