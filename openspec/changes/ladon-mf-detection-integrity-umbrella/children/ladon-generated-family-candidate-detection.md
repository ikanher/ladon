# `ladon-generated-family-candidate-detection`

Adds path-independent, advisory structural candidates for regular module families
while keeping configured generated provenance authoritative.

- Dependencies: the
  `ladon-report-coverage-and-snapshot-integrity#coverage-foundation` milestone,
  `ladon-declaration-and-audit-integrity`, and
  `ladon-project-ownership-and-generated-calibration`.
- Enables: candidate-family inspection, bounded family region-registration rows,
  and policy suggestions.
- Excludes: automatic provenance, generator or freshness claims, policy writes,
  target-specific path rules, or treating target-owned as handwritten.
- Exit: portable contiguous-family positives and regular-handwritten negatives are
  deterministic, predicate-witness-explained with exact bounded feature-key
  coverage, leave the configured population unchanged, and emit no complete
  review-region object.
