# `ladon-scope-and-join-integrity`

Corrects cross-capability evidence semantics for selected scopes, discovered
inventory membership, reachability roots, and composite correlations.

- Dependencies: the
  `ladon-report-coverage-and-snapshot-integrity#coverage-foundation` milestone,
  `ladon-analysis-root-and-scope-contract`,
  `ladon-signal-correctness`, and `ladon-architecture-correlator`.
- Enables: trustworthy collision co-reachability, inspection boundaries, and
  coverage-aware producer registration for later review-region synthesis.
- Excludes: root selection policy, mathematical relevance inference, import
  rewriting, or new smell taxonomies.
- Exit: portable positive and negative fixtures distinguish known out-of-slice
  imports from missing modules, keep rootless inventory reachability unavailable,
  and suppress every composite whose component evidence lacks its declared
  structural join.
