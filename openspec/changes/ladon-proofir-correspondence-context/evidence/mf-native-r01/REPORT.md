# Native MF evidence qualification

2026-11-16. Exposed, supplied-proof development using retained MF sources and
read-only compiled dependencies. This is not a fresh audit, automatic discovery,
independent review or comparative benefit measurement.

## Inputs and meaning

The prior [two-case trace](../mf-trace-r01/REPORT.md) owns the original source
findings and historical ordinary-Lean evidence. This exercise qualifies native
production and inspection for selected applications of those existing results.

**fDP.** `project/Fdp.lean` preserves the entire original finite countermodel,
then adds a small application wrapper and the explicit negation of the selected
source consequence. The attributed source formula asserts the factor-5/3 bound
for the retained neighboring datasets. Its dummy Nat binder only makes the
wrapper an application; it changes no probability claim. `neighbor` aliases the
original primed dataset name. The conclusion-transfer query asks whether the
checked strict reverse inequality implies the claimed bound. A refutation of
that transfer must remain distinct from the separately established finite
counterexample, whose original premises and sampled law are retained above it.
The source verdict is conditional on inherited increasing-index ordering; the
manuscript's intended ordering remains unresolved.

**DP-Adam.** `adam-project/AdamCase.lean` preserves the entire original general
constructor, then groups its primitive inputs and hypotheses in `AdamNative.Inputs`.
No convergence conclusion is a field. `commonLaw` contains one existential law
outside the conjunction of the two actual process-limit claims. `bridge` applies
`paired_source_convergence`; `selected` applies that bridge. A separate original
scoped extraction, `adam-slice.json`, records the larger theorem's actual
`source_convergence` application. The native wrapper makes this existing claim
expressible through a named predicate; inspect does not automatically unfold
that predicate or certify its source attribution. Readers must inspect its
preserved definition. This does not establish finite-run equivalence, a uniform
sampling-rate threshold, equality of accountants or simultaneous Malladi beta
scaling. The full exposition remains unaudited.

The source anchors, typed symbols, maps, supplied terms and generated goals live
in `fdp/` and `adam/`. Interpretation and object-map checks establish typing;
they do not prove prose fidelity. This qualification uses the explicit source
readings already exposed in earlier work.

## Execution and repairs

All operations use the local Ladon entrypoint through `run.py`. Local projects
link existing compiled dependency roots read-only; no donor build or mutation
is performed. Primary inputs and direct compiled imports are hash-bound in
`INPUTS-r03.json` and `ADAM-INPUTS.json`. Earlier failed wrapper variants and all
process receipts are retained. The corrected wrappers preserve the original
proof files as exact prefixes.

Initial outer checks exhausted 300 seconds. The user authorized 1800 seconds;
`TIMEOUT-REVISION.json` records the extension and common remaining deadline.
The 12-GiB process-tree RSS cap, 8-MiB captured-output bound and individual helper
limits were unchanged. The first extended attempts ended after 488.04 and 441.28
seconds with a collection-limit error, not a timeout, memory event or Lean
refutation. Their peak RSS was approximately 9.00 and 9.45 GiB.

The real closures exposed generic 10000-item canonical bounds at three sites:
correspondence envelopes, standalone slice projection, and captured inventories
inside receipt hashes. The repair admits the existing 32768-module bound only
at those exact paths and recognized root shapes. Other collections, descendant
rows, byte/depth limits, semantic validation and replay/trust checks are unchanged.
Previously accepted canonical bytes and identities do not change. Runtime
snapshots retain each phase; `RUNTIME-RECEIPT-FIX.json` identifies the final retry.
The compiler and proof-checker modules were not modified.

Boundary tests first failed before their corresponding fixes. The final relevant
suite passes **184 tests** (`PYTEST-RECEIPT.txt`); Ruff and strict Ladon quality
pass on the touched canonicalizer and regression file. No independent review
is claimed. The new tests cover overflow, unrelated/nested paths, invalid
recognition tags, row contents, byte bounds and exact hash-byte compatibility.
