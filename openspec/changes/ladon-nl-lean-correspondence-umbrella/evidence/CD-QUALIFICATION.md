## Implemented behavior

The opt-in `scoped-local-argument-v1` slice traverses the actual Lean theorem
value, preserving lambda/let contexts, distinct branch occurrences, nested
witness ownership, and all ordered proof arguments. It closes each provider
under the exact observed context and replays provider obligations separately
from the downstream conditional and original prose premise supply. A missing
predecessor can refute correspondence while the formal conditional remains
supported. Method and source fidelity stay separate from endpoint validity.

Closed structural application DAGs must match their ordered argument
projections. Rebuilding and extracting before/after checking rejects changed
contexts. The checker captures its implementation identity at load, rejects
changes before/during replay, and passes that captured identity to native
check-run receipts. Historical checked bundles remain stored evidence without
fresh replay authority.

V3 investigation reuses interpretation/prepared/checked native bundles, exports
sanitized source/goal inputs, preserves immutable first responses, and stores
corrections separately. Mathematical review, source fidelity, method, and
formal-link completion remain distinct. Missing counterparts, incomplete
inventory, unsupported forms, and budget exhaustion stay visible. V1/v2 remain
available. Rust explicitly rejects the two new unsupported artifact kinds;
this does not claim cross-language support for them.

Incoming declaration evidence ownership is validated before selection;
references are retargeted after internal projection ordering. Genuine malformed
containers remain rejected. The unavailable export-surface flag remains
explicitly rejected and is absent from ordinary help. The original broad
producer failure was not reproduced; the minimized internal-reorder defect
was reproduced and fixed.
