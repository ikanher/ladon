## Why

- Integrate v3 claims, observations, derivations, attachments, and coverage into theorem dossiers, triage, consumers, constructors, and proof-search planning.

- Update documentation and skills, add installed-wheel tests, and calibrate on Matrix-Factorization while enforcing complete separation from Quux.
- Publish a release evidence manifest proving legacy routes are absent and native-v3 routes are complete.
