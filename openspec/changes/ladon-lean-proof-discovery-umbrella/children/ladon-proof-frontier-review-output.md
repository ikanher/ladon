# `ladon-proof-frontier-review-output`

Combines proof-discovery evidence into a bounded, revision-aware frontier and source-first review packet.

- Dependencies: consumers/coverage, representation/scale, goal probes, packet evidence, and theorem-surface changelog.
- Enables: one integrated view of viable, blocked, and rejected proof routes.
- Excludes: declaring the theorem proved or embedding Matrix-Factorization-specific conclusions.
- Exit: all six portable acceptance scenarios and optional fingerprinted Matrix-Factorization observations pass.
