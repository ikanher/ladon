# `ladon-premise-difference-and-adapter-routing`

Explains why a candidate does or does not apply, searches missing premises, and records Lean-checked adapter routes.

- Dependencies: completed type-directed search and supervised Lean execution.
- Enables: constructor coverage and representation-aware routing.
- Excludes: automatic proof repair or unverified adapter claims.
- Exit: stable route cards distinguish substitutions, obligations, residual differences, and rejected routes.
