The exact profile limit is inverted by bracketing the calibrated noise between
$\Delta/(\sqrt{2\log T}+s_{E,\delta}\pm\eta)$ for each $\eta>0$.
The general profile limit and this inversion are proved in the appendix. For $T\geq E$, the capped schedule has
$q_T=\min(1,E/T)=E/T$, and its calibrated standard deviation is exactly
$\sigma_{E,T}$. Specializing the general offset theorem to this schedule and
writing $T=E+k$, $k\in\mathbb{N}$, therefore gives the displayed offset limit.
