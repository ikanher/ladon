The divergence is not a consequence of the growing horizon alone. The raw noise
is recalibrated for each horizon and tends to zero; what matters is its rate of
decay, since $v_{E,T}=T\sigma_{E,T}^2/E^2$. The theorem shows that this rate is
$\sigma_{E,T}\sim\Delta/\sqrt{2\log T}$, giving
$v_{E,T}\sim\Delta^2T/(2E^2\log T)\to\infty$. This supplies eventual dominance
by full batch. The conclusion depends on the public output: average-only
calibration instead admits the uniform bound in
Proposition~\ref{prop:average-uniform-bound}.
