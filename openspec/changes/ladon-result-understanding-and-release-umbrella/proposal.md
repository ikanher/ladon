## Why

An accepted Lean declaration does not by itself show that a paper's intended claim was formalized faithfully, that its assumptions are understood, or that an explanation and attribution have been reviewed. Ladon can help humans and LLMs investigate these questions by connecting its existing theorem evidence and lineage to mathematical claims and reading material, following the technical opportunities identified in [Responsible Release of AI-Generated Mathematics](https://agmai.org/general-sep29/) (September 29, 2026, especially §2.B Step I.1–5 and Step II).

## Current objective and next commitment

Help AI-assisted mathematics become understandable, checkable, attributable and
reusable by the mathematical community. Command uptake and request cost are
supporting engineering observations, not the governing success criterion.

The r08 review accepts r68 maintenance and closes that optimization cycle.
The subsequent real fixed-epoch divergence question produced an explanatory
correction accepted by r09 as a narrow editorial contribution. The refined
companion and proposed manuscript transition are ready; this cycle is closed
with manuscript adoption pending. The [current frontier](tasks.md) owns the

## What Changes

- Put conventional explanation, precise support and reader correction first.
  Design decision 14 owns the bounded workflow and stopping point; prior
  experiments remain historical and the benefit gate remains unmet.
- Close accepted canonical-reuse maintenance. Standalone interface, extractor,
  release-feature and optimization expansion remains frozen unless actual use
  exposes a specific recurring handoff or error worth addressing.
- Introduce a versioned result manifest linking immutable informal claim revisions to exact, environment-qualified Lean declarations, with separately attributable correspondence reviews and explicit unresolved links.
- Add per-result formalization dossiers that expose statements, assumptions, checker observations, external frontiers, obligations, freshness, and coverage without compressing these into a single verification badge.
- Attach bounded proof reading guides, explanations, and literature references to exact claims and declarations; distinguish structural navigation, generated exposition, human review, and attribution assertions.
- Record voluntarily supplied research-process provenance and attempt populations, including failures, incomplete capture, model identity, prompts or disclosure limitations, process summaries, runtime, and cost estimates.
- Export portable, revisioned result bundles with integrity manifests, optional existing theorem capsules, and versioned checks for explicitly selected community artifact profiles.
- Historical engineering evaluation scope: collect qualitative LLM usage feedback on assumptions, formalization gaps, and reusable lemmas, ending in an explicit application to a new goal and a faithfully scoped paragraph. Evaluate application and exposition in separate short sessions before combining them; use the same guide/evidence and competent Lean tooling in the comparison, with operation recording outside reader responsibilities. Quantitative metrics and human cohorts remain deferred. This is not an instruction to repeat the experiments; decision 14 now governs the real reader round.
- Use the completed fixed-epoch exposition baseline to require component-level omission reasons, explicit strict/closed and transcript/average-only differences, and separate manuscript/archive/working-tree identities. Retain its unavailable operations as baseline outcomes, not implemented capabilities.
- Sequence six bounded implementation children with authority-bearing integration after the existing authority-safe integration and verified-discovery exits; permit independent experimental artifact validation before those exits. The original engineering milestone is retained as historical scope. Further provenance/profile extensions are deferred; they do not follow automatically from completion of a companion or correction round.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `ladon-result-claim-correspondence`: Exact claim/declaration links, revision binding, correspondence reviews, and explicit differences or uncertainty.
- `ladon-result-formalization-dossier`: Bounded result inspection with independent formalization, checking, assumption, review, and coverage evidence.
- `ladon-proof-reading-guides`: Source-linked explanations, reading paths, reusable-lemma navigation, citation attachments, and review attribution.
- `ladon-research-process-provenance`: Disclosed generation records and coverage-aware problem/attempt ledgers.
- `ladon-result-release-bundles`: Portable result packages, revision history, integrity validation, and optional pinned community artifact profiles.
- `ladon-result-understanding-evaluation`: Qualitative usage scenarios, reproducible LLM feedback, and evidence-bounded capability readiness; comparative metrics deferred.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Existing ProofIR observation, attachment, identity, graph, and CLI contracts remain authoritative and are consumed without a new ProofIR family or wire major.

## Impact

Expected integration points are the ordinary CLI, exact theorem evidence and lineage queries, semantic evidence registry, bounded projections, theorem capsules, readiness records, and installed-distribution tests. A separate result-manifest schema references canonical evidence; disposable indexes remain projections. No mandatory network service, model provider, or additional proof extractor is introduced.

The source document informs requirements; it is not a Ladon certification standard. Funding, scholarly judgment, public repository operation, equitable model access, and the document's opposition to proprietary-model mathematical research are outside Ladon's enforcement scope. Preparing this umbrella does not change existing feature readiness or authorize implementation to skip prerequisite gates. See `sources.md` and `children/dependency-ledger.json` for traceability and sequencing.
