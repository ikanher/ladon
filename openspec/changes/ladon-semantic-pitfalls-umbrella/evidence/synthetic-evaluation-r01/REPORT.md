## What was validated

The author constructed passages over the unchanged local original Lean capsule.
The reviewer independently checked the proposed interpretations, witnesses under
the passages' actual assumptions, stochastic realizability, positive controls,
and multiple defensible ambiguity readings. The two faults were judged distinct
from each other and from the exposed development example. Labels were not
accepted solely because a statement lacked a proof.

This is source-mathematics review and scoped inspection of the original Lean
counterpart. No new Lean counterexample or oracle certificate was compiled.
The [original capsule's successful replay](../synthetic-challenge-r01/REPORT.md)
is separate evidence about the unchanged formal development. It does not certify
the translation or truth of a mutated passage.

The independent review found a missing standalone-context premise in the
submitted revision. The author clarified the projection, retained the submitted
version and the review, and obtained acceptance of v3. Earlier construction
drafts also remain in custody. The accepted set has five units; earlier rejected
versions are construction history, not extra evaluated units.
