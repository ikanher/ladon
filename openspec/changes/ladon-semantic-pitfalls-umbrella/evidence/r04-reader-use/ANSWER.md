## Answer

**Not as support for the finite batch comparison `thm:pair`.** The retained certificate establishes a useful but different result: for each packaged input, two families of clipped private Adam *path laws* converge to one common asymptotic law under a shared fixed-beta model, where the second family changes the Poisson sampling probability and both update-count sequences tend to infinity. The current MF theorem compares two particular **finite** recursions with a simultaneous beta transformation, finite bias corrections, a quantitative expected squared-gradient risk bound, and separately calibrated finite privacy. The certificate does not establish that comparison or its finite error bound.

It could be cited as a candidate source for an asymptotic common-law component only after a new, explicit bridge resolves the model differences. In particular the checked pair theorem does not vary beta, while the finite MF pair does. The stored mapping itself is marked `attributed-unreviewed`, and the retained source correspondence is not certified. This is a certificate-support conclusion, not a claim that either source theorem is true or false.
