# Proposal

## Why

The completed 90-minute audit reached a concrete correspondence discrepancy but left its local derivation at “appears consistent.” It accumulated 552 captures and only 13 contracts. The next useful improvement is to test the disputed implication and prioritize its unresolved steps.

## What Changes

- Add opt-in version-2 investigation plans with source-bound derivation steps, discrepancy-first scheduling and independently attributed challenges bound to the exact reviewed context.
- Add a small exact rational witness checker for authored scalar implications; check assumptions and the claimed conclusion without evaluating code or treating finite success as a universal proof.
- Exercise the public commands with independently authored small cases and faithful controls, then an independent challenger whose first responses exclude the answer key.
- Preserve both completed research-paper runs. Stop after implementation, consumer checks and the bounded small-case exercise; no further full-paper retest in this context.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `local-derivation-challenges`: Decompose and challenge local proof steps, prioritize discrepancies and replay exact rational counterexamples with explicit interpretation limits.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Existing version-1 investigation and closed ProofIR/result contracts remain compatible.

## Impact

Ladon investigation runtime/CLI, new dependency-free arithmetic and derivation modules, focused tests, generic documentation and packaged skill. Existing ordinary Lean checks remain available for claims outside the rational fragment. No new dependencies, model service integration or automatic mathematical text parser.
