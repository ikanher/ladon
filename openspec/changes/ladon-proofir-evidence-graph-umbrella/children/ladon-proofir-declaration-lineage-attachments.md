# `ladon-proofir-declaration-lineage-attachments`

Attaches surfaces to unique source-compatible declaration IDs and overlays those
attachments on compatible active theorem lineage without merging authorities.

- Starts after: surface/replay and obligation DAG portable exits.
- Enables: theorem-first and artifact-first stored evidence queries.
- Excludes: first-match, fuzzy, prose, module, or proximity-based theorem joins.
- Exit: exact/ambiguous/stale/rebuild/lineage cases and the CDC negative oracle pass.
