# `ladon-proofir-obligation-dag-ingestion-and-queries`

Normalizes admitted ProofIR v2 obligation graphs and checker witnesses, then
answers bounded forward/reverse routes with recursive SQL.

- Starts after: catalog and surface/replay portable exits.
- Enables: contextual theorem evidence and final CLI route queries.
- Excludes: Lean lineage edges, exhaustive paths, and checker authority promotion.
- Exit: graph integrity, indexes/query plans, cycle/cap behavior, and exact mixed-
  authority CDC route oracles pass.
