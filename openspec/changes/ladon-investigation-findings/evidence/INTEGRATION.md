## Established behavior

- Status and fresh progress-v2 receipts retain attributed legacy statement
  discrepancies, method differences and uncertainties. Captured claims do not
  inherit a completed review from a leftover judgment. Existing v2 arithmetic
  findings retain their distinct checker authority.
- Native review projections retain classifications, first responses, correction
  histories and effective conflicts. Corrections preserve their original reviewer
  label. Neither a completed review nor stored links establishes paper falsity.
- `observe` captures exact source and optional formal-source spans without native
  extraction. `inspect-observations`, sanitized `challenge`, `ingest` and
  `reconcile` preserve immutable source/response identities. Pending observations
  enter the actual queue; resolved observation-only work can finish with formal
  correspondence explicitly incomplete.
- `report` retains compatible supplementary branches under one original scope,
  clock and budget. It preserves observations, first/corrected reviews, conflicts,
  plan provenance and actual pending queues across all review dimensions.
  Identical plans deduplicate; immutable forks and incompatible clocks reject.
  Input, output and record-population bounds are explicit.
- Coverage counts authored survey and argument-review records separately and
  keeps whole-paper certification false. Reports are read-only views, not merged
  plans or terminal receipts. Historical progress-v1 projection remains readable
  against unchanged sources, plan and recorded time; old receipts are not rewritten.

The [user guide](../../../../docs/INVESTIGATION_FINDINGS.md) gives the ordinary
CLI workflow using the current checkout. The frozen portable CLI test exercises
capture → inspection → sanitized challenge → attributed review → finalize →
supplementary report and stale/overwrite refusal. It requires no model service,
research checkout or Lean rebuild.
