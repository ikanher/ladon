# Proposal

## Why

The current mathematical contract producer cannot represent typed Real-valued bound estimates. A general extension needs to distinguish an observed statement shape from checked admission to mathematical rules; source names and numerical losses cannot supply that admission.

## What Changes

- Introduce a separate closed observation for typed estimates and a separately versioned Real-bound mathematical profile, preserving `norm-scale-v1` unchanged.
- Retain full binder scope, predicate and operator applications, guards, function/set identities and literal losses. Admit semantics only through source-bound Lean evidence.
- Integrate admission, comparison and native consumers in stages, with unsupported consumers failing explicitly.
- Qualify generality with unrelated predicates/operators and varied losses, then replay the original informed cases as secondary integration measurements.

The user authorized this extension on 2026-10-25 and required generality. This is a narrow amendment to the parent runtime freeze, not authorization for a new paper audit. Acceptance requirements are in [the specification](specs/ladon-real-bound-contracts/spec.md). First delivery is source-shape observation and inspection; it does not satisfy the later mathematical admission or extraction gates.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `ladon-real-bound-contracts`: Typed Real-bound observations, checked predicate/rule admission, and conservative consumer integration.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Historical contract meanings and identities remain unchanged.

## Impact

Formal observation, proof-contract CLI/inspection, new bounded recognizer, profile and admission modules, Lean law helpers, closed native records and direct consumers. No new Python dependency, Quux dependency, cache, automatic prose interpretation or full-paper proof is introduced. Parent task 2.1 and evaluation gates remain independently governed.
