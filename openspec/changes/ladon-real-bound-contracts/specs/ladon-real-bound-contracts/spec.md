## Purpose

Preserve typed Real-bound estimates and admit their mathematical operations through explicit, source-bound evidence across unrelated Lean developments.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Typed shape observation
The system SHALL preserve a bounded, closed observation of a typed estimate with ordered binders, exact predicate/operator applications, input and set references, literal order shift, guards and expression origins. Shape observation SHALL carry no semantic contract authority. Unsupported shape or polarity SHALL retain the source observation and a named residual.

#### Scenario: Unrelated bound predicates
- **WHEN** two unrelated developments use different predicate and operator names with supported shapes and losses 0 and 7
- **THEN** observations retain their distinct applications and actual shifts without requiring a target-specific name or offset

#### Scenario: Uniformity changes
- **WHEN** an existential constant moves after a function or set binder
- **THEN** its recorded permission scope changes and no uniformity over that earlier binder is inferred

#### Scenario: Unsupported symbolic shift
- **WHEN** an input order contains an unfixed second index
- **THEN** the source is retained without replacing that index by a numeric loss

### Requirement: Checked predicate admission
A mathematical contract SHALL bind its predicate and exact application to checked definition/equivalence evidence in the pinned source environment. Unchecked admission SHALL remain an explicit obligation. Naming, string matching and syntactic shape alone SHALL NOT admit derivative or norm semantics.

#### Scenario: Same name and different definition
- **WHEN** a predicate definition changes while retaining its name and apparent argument shape
- **THEN** old admission evidence is rejected and the new observation remains unadmitted until checked

### Requirement: Real rule evidence
Each enabled Real-bound transfer or dominance rule SHALL require checked laws and its actual side conditions. Order-prefix weakening, bound monotonicity and multiplication/composition SHALL NOT inherit the natural-scalar profile's proofs. Guard removal and dependency narrowing SHALL require separate evidence.

#### Scenario: Missing nonnegative multiplier
- **WHEN** a composition lacks the required nonnegative scalar guard
- **THEN** the rule is unavailable and the missing guard is reported

#### Scenario: Pointwise coefficient weakening preserves the output context
- **WHEN** a source-bound coefficient rule weakens an actual output product to `L*C` under `K ≤ L` and `0 ≤ C`
- **THEN** the checked implication retains the original predicate parameters, object, set and order, including their dependence on `K`, and does not infer a uniform existential witness

#### Scenario: Admission fails before a coefficient rule
- **WHEN** fresh admission is incomplete or reaches its resource limit
- **THEN** the coefficient worker is not launched, available admission receipts and diagnostics are preserved, and the result remains unresolved without fresh rule authority

### Requirement: Explicit consumer dispatch
Typed observations and admitted Real contracts SHALL use distinct closed versions. Historical `norm-scale-v1` records and digests SHALL remain unchanged. Each consumer SHALL support the exact version and evidence or explicitly reject it; unadmitted observations SHALL NOT enter comparison or propagation as semantic contracts.

#### Scenario: Old propagation receives an observation
- **WHEN** a shape observation is supplied as a mathematical contract
- **THEN** validation rejects it rather than interpreting its numeric shift under the Nat rules

#### Scenario: Conditional composition preserves its premises
- **WHEN** two admitted source applications have compatible typed contexts and Lean checks their exact middle application and scalar products
- **THEN** the composition result retains both guarded transfer steps as explicit assumptions and the actual outer endpoints, without inferring source theorem proofs or uniform existential witnesses

#### Scenario: Unsupported dependent composition
- **WHEN** an order, object or coefficient substitution would change a retained dependent data type or require inherited proof-valued data
- **THEN** composition remains unresolved rather than dropping that dependency

### Requirement: Exact compatible comparison
Real-bound comparison SHALL require compatible typed contexts, predicates, operators, input/set identities, guards and constant scope. Numeric shifts SHALL NOT establish a relationship between different endpoints or norm interpretations.

#### Scenario: Distinct operators have equal loss
- **WHEN** two estimates have the same shift but different operators
- **THEN** comparison reports an unresolved counterpart relationship without semantic dominance

### Requirement: Durable observation and evidence
Public production and inspection SHALL preserve source snapshots, expression origins, unresolved admissions and rule evidence with bounded immutable output. Source, environment, implementation or profile drift SHALL invalidate reuse. Native storage and report consumers SHALL preserve supported admission references and residuals.

#### Scenario: Altered extracted shift
- **WHEN** a saved observation's shift is changed while its source snapshot is retained
- **THEN** inspection rejects the changed observation on exact replay, even if its outer identity was recomputed

#### Scenario: Native conditional rule history
- **WHEN** coefficient or transfer rule evidence is saved as a native analysis
- **THEN** storage retains the exact conditional goal and evidence owners, inspection remains historical without fresh authority, and replay requires fresh admission and checking under one shared deadline

### Requirement: General qualification and honest metrics
Qualification SHALL exercise independently named generic fixtures before informed source replays and count observations, admitted contracts and false promotions separately. A paper-specific norm bridge SHALL remain unresolved until separately checked. Known-source capability SHALL NOT count as unfamiliar-source discovery.

#### Scenario: Original fixed-loss cases become observable
- **WHEN** two informed estimates yield typed observations but lack checked admissions
- **THEN** the report credits two observations and zero admitted contracts, retaining the semantic extraction and paper-bridge gates
