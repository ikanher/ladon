# First source-bound regularity consumer

The first regularity application-probe consumer has actual discrepancy/control
proof evidence. Its **19-outcome source-check sequence passed in 281.219
seconds**, inside one declared 1,800-second window. Independent review accepted
the evidence with the ordering limitation described below.
This is partial progress toward probes task 1.1. The child remains **1/8** and
the umbrella **2/12**; no additional task closes from this first family.

The new public `probe`, `check-probe`, `probe-profile`, and native `inspect`
paths bind two native contracts to their original interpretation owners and
the same actual closed Lean application. The template matches the original
typed natural-number estimate ASTs, rather than constructing a proposition
from regularity labels. Native plans use `proofir.contract-analysis`, stage
`probes`. Their open goals and absent checker authority remain separate from
actual check results. See [the public contract](PUBLIC-API.md).

## Remaining scope

Only the closed, premise-free two-Nat-binder regularity fragment is supported.
Constant uniformity, witness dependence, restricted-domain and inequality
direction templates remain unsupported. Native checked-result/reviewer export,
result reuse, rational routes, the full five-fault/five-control evaluation and
broader investigation integration remain unfinished.

Source fidelity stays attributed-unreviewed, whole-method authority stays none,
and mathematical falsity stays not-established. This constructed example does
not establish manuscript-error detection or detection benefit. The matched
no-beam baseline remains preserved, held-out C has zero admitted cases, and no
beam policy has been introduced. Next implement and separately qualify the
remaining typed families against their original propositions.
