# `ladon-signal-correctness-hardening`

Owns the reproduced missing-import, graph-population, lexical declaration,
namespace-drift, proof-family similarity, and duplicate-promotion defects.

- Dependencies: reconciliation transfers residual ownership from overlapping
  historical packets; report v2 receives corrected metadata; the installed CLI
  contract supplies the stable regression invocation.
- Enables: trustworthy default findings and benchmark labels.
- Excludes: new heuristic families and Lean-elaborated dependency extraction.
- Exit: every defect has a CLI-level positive and intentional-negative
  regression, with raw evidence and authority preserved; benchmark manifests
  and new-family promotion remain downstream.
