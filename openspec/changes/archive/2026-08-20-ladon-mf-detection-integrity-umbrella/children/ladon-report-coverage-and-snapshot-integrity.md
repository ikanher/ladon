# `ladon-report-coverage-and-snapshot-integrity`

Makes nested projection and atlas completeness explicit, preserves evidence-class
representation, and renders all requested formats from one source-stable analysis
model.

- Dependencies: `ladon-large-inventory-scale-contract`, `ladon-report-contract-v2`,
  `ladon-cli-execution-contract`, `ladon-installed-reportset-workflow`, and
  `ladon-review-regions`.
- Milestones: `coverage-foundation` establishes the shared collection model and
  generic registries before evidence producers; `snapshot-and-region-integration`
  exclusively synthesizes concrete review regions and closes multi-render parity
  after all producer packets close on their registration rows and actions.
- Enables: after `coverage-foundation`, every other integrity child can publish
  coverage-bearing rows; final completion enables trustworthy exhaustive queries,
  severity-aware summaries, integrity review routing, and comparable multi-format
  artifacts.
- Excludes: multiple stdout documents, hidden reanalysis per renderer, silent
  acceptance of source drift, or duplicated atlas/report engines.
- Exit: coverage arithmetic composes at every collection, stratified projections
  preserve evidence classes, incomplete atlas queries cannot claim exhaustiveness,
  and injected drift cannot yield a stable complete snapshot.
