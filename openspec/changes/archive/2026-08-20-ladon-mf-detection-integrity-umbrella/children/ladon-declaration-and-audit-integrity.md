# `ladon-declaration-and-audit-integrity`

Integrates namespace-aware lexical declaration candidates with continuation-aware
audit extraction and evidence-based audit-facade roles.

- Dependencies: the
  `ladon-report-coverage-and-snapshot-integrity#coverage-foundation` milestone,
  `ladon-scope-and-join-integrity`,
  `ladon-declaration-source-evidence`, `ladon-elaborated-declaration-surface`,
  `ladon-lean-audit-command-surface`, and
  `ladon-common-layer-and-facade-quality`.
- Enables: collision and audit region-registration rows, audit-subject
  navigation, generated-family structural evidence, and declaration inspection.
- Excludes: Lean name-resolution claims from text, theorem/build verdicts, filename
  heuristics, or a replacement Lean extractor.
- Exit: portable namespace/private/duplicate and multiline-command fixtures
  produce stable authority-bounded candidates; command-only audit modules cannot
  remain pure barrels or receive audit-only public-API pressure; complete
  review-region objects remain report-owned.
