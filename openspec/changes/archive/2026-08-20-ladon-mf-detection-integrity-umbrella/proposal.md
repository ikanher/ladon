## Why

A fresh ordinary-CLI pass over the Matrix-Factorization repository showed that
Ladon's import inventory is useful, but several promoted conclusions are not
supported by their evidence and most of the declaration, audit, generated-family,
proof-mechanism, and resource surface is either misclassified or inaccessible.
The alpha product now needs detection-integrity gates that make large-repository
review trustworthy and navigable before more signal kinds are added.

## What Changes

- Correct scope accounting so imports that exist in the discovered inventory but
  lie outside a selected owner slice are reported as known boundaries, not missing
  modules, and so inventory analysis does not silently invent a reachability root.
- Require promoted composite findings to carry a structural join between their
  component signals instead of combining unrelated global maxima.
- Add namespace-aware lexical declaration identity and collision candidates,
  including exact-source duplicates and co-reachable shard overlap, with explicit
  lexical authority and optional Lean confirmation.
- Parse supported multiline audit commands, classify command-only audit modules as
  audit surfaces rather than generic pure barrels, and preserve containing-module,
  referenced-owner, backend, and result authority separately.
- Detect path-independent generated-looking module families from advisory
  structural evidence while keeping configured provenance authoritative and
  removing the unsupported equivalence between target-owned and handwritten;
  count every observed feature key before a fixed strongest-12 projection per
  kind/version, with exact coverage and stable evidence references.
- Expose ordinary, caller-independent inspection of modules, declarations,
  imports, audits, options, resources, and proof-mechanism summaries with stable
  filters, pagination, source anchors, authority, and completeness metadata.
- Aggregate option and resource review surfaces by lexical scope and family.
  Only normalized unlimited resource settings or matches from explicit configured
  policy may be labeled pressure; finite values remain navigation evidence absent
  such policy.
- Make every projection and atlas consumer disclose visible, total, omitted, and
  completeness state; use stratified evidence selection and severity-aware text
  summaries instead of generic first-N truncation.
- Render multiple requested formats from one immutable analysis result and reject
  or diagnose source drift rather than letting equivalent outputs describe
  different repository states.
- Add portable positive and negative regression fixtures plus optional
  fingerprinted Matrix-Factorization observational acceptance. Production code
  and required CI MUST NOT hard-code or depend on that repository.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `ladon-scope-and-join-integrity`: Defines full-inventory-aware scope
  classification, truthful reachability roots, and structural evidence joins for
  composite findings.
- `ladon-declaration-and-audit-integrity`: Defines namespace-aware lexical
  declaration candidates, collision evidence, multiline audit parsing, audit
  facade roles, and authority-preserving subject ownership.
- `ladon-generated-family-candidate-detection`: Defines advisory,
  path-independent generated-looking family evidence without replacing configured
  project provenance.
- `ladon-analysis-inspection-surface`: Defines ordinary CLI inspection, paginated
  source-index access, lexical proof-mechanism and scope summaries, and option and
  resource navigation.
- `ladon-report-coverage-and-snapshot-integrity`: Defines projection and atlas
  coverage, stratified review evidence, immutable multi-format rendering, source
  drift detection, and review-region routing.

### Modified Capabilities

None. The new integrity capabilities tighten and compose the existing pipeline,
root/scope, audit-command, generated-calibration, findings, report, and installed
workflow contracts without silently replacing their authorities.

## Impact

- Affected code includes scope planning and module-DAG summaries, architecture
  correlation, lexical source indexing, audit parsing and facade classification,
  generated-family calibration, report projection and rendering, atlas ingestion,
  CLI dispatch, cache/fingerprint handling, review-region synthesis, and portable
  fixtures.
- Public behavior gains general-purpose inspection operations and more explicit
  coverage and authority fields. There is no LLM-specific command, output mode,
  threshold, or default.
- Existing finding kinds whose evidence is not structurally joined may be
  suppressed or downgraded; existing `pure_barrel` and “handwritten” labels may
  change where stronger role evidence exists.
- Text-only analysis remains non-elaborating. It may identify lexical candidates,
  navigation pressure, and proof-mechanism syntax, but it does not establish Lean
  declaration identity, theorem truth, proof success, axiom closure, or measured
  runtime.
- Required tests use tracked portable fixtures. The Matrix-Factorization tree
  remains read-only, optional observational evidence with recorded source and
  tool fingerprints.
